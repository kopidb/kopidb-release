# ☕ kopidb

**Find coffee by flavour.** kopidb is an Android app for discovering coffee shops
by the tastes you like: fruity, floral, sweet, nutty, earthy, spicy or sour.

This page is where you download the app. It is an early test version, so expect
the odd rough edge.

## What you can do

- **Browse a map of coffee shops** and tap one to see its menu and beans.
- **Explore by flavour.** Pick the tasting notes you enjoy and see which shops
  serve coffee that matches.
- **Save your favourite shops** with the heart button so they are easy to find
  again.
- **Get directions** to a café in Google Maps.
- **Help improve the listings.** Vote on bean flavours, scan a coffee bag to add
  its beans, suggest menu changes, or add a missing shop from a Google Maps link.
  Suggestions are checked before they appear for everyone.
- **Dark mode** is in Profile → Settings.

## Install it

You need an Android phone. There is no iPhone version yet.

1. On your phone, open this page and tap **`kopidb.apk`** above, then tap
   **Download** (or **View raw**).
2. Open the downloaded file.
3. If your phone warns that it can't install apps from this source, tap
   **Settings**, allow it, then go back and tap **Install**.
4. Open **kopidb** and sign in with your email. You'll be sent a code to enter.

The app asks for your location so it can show nearby shops on the map. Your
location stays on your phone.

## Updating

Download the newest `kopidb.apk` from this page and install it over the top. You
don't need to uninstall first, and your account and saved shops stay as they are.
