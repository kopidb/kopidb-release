# Delete your kopidb account

You can delete your kopidb account, and the information kept with it, at any time.

## In the app

1. Open kopidb and sign in.
2. Go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account.
3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the app

Email kopi.db.business@gmail.com from the email address your account uses and ask for it to be deleted. We will confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- your account, email address and sign-in details;
- your display name and photo;
- your saved shops, votes and blocked list;
- suggestions of yours that are still waiting for review or were not accepted.

## What is kept

- Suggestions that were approved stay in the public catalogue, no longer linked to you.
- Reports you made stay with the admins, no longer linked to you.
- Copies in routine backups and server logs are removed on our hosting provider's normal schedule.

See the kopidb privacy policy for more about what we collect and why.
