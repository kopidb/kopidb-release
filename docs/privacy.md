# kopidb privacy policy

Effective 3 October 2026.

kopidb is an app for finding coffee shops by flavour. This policy explains what kopidb ("we") collects when you use it, why, who else is involved, and how to delete it. We collect only what the app needs to work. kopidb has no advertising, no analytics or tracking libraries, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your email address, and a password if you choose to set one. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash by our sign-in provider; we cannot read them. If you add an authenticator app, its setup key is stored with your account so your codes can be checked.

**Your profile.** A display name and a photo, if you add them. Both are optional and both are public: anyone can see them, including people who are not signed in, and they may be shown with your contributions.

**What you contribute.** Suggestions you send (new shops, drinks, beans, flavour notes, address corrections and removal requests, with any reason you type), your votes on a bean's flavours, and reports you make about content or people. Suggestions are linked to your account so admins can review them. Once approved, the information becomes part of the public catalogue.

**What you keep in the app.** Your saved shops, your list of blocked people, and your light or dark theme choice.

**Technical records.** Like most online services, our hosting provider logs requests, including IP address and time, to keep the service secure and working.

## What stays on your device

**Location.** If you allow it, kopidb uses your location to show where you are on the map. Your position is used on the device only and is never sent to our servers. Map images are downloaded for the area you look at, so the map providers below can see which area that is.

**Camera and photos.** When you scan a bean pack, the text is read on your device. The picture is not uploaded; only the details you confirm are sent as a suggestion. A photo you choose as your profile photo is uploaded.

**Fingerprint or face unlock.** Your phone does the check and tells kopidb only whether it passed. kopidb never receives your fingerprint or face data. Your sign-in session is kept in your device's secure storage.

## Who else is involved

kopidb relies on a few outside services. Each receives only what it needs, and each sees your IP address as part of any internet request.

- **Supabase** hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage, on servers in Sydney, Australia. It also sends sign-in emails.
- **CARTO and OpenFreeMap** provide the map's style and images for the areas you view.
- **Photon by komoot** looks up addresses when you add a shop or correct an address. It receives the place's name, address or coordinates, not yours.
- **Google.** When you paste a Google Maps link, kopidb opens that link to read the place's details. Directions open Google Maps with the shop's location. Text on bean packs is read by Google's ML Kit library on your device; the library may send Google diagnostic and usage information about itself, but not your pictures or the text.

Map data is from OpenStreetMap contributors.

## Why we use it

- To sign you in and keep your account secure.
- To show the catalogue, your saved shops and your votes.
- To review, publish and moderate contributions, and to act on reports.
- To prevent spam and abuse, for example by limiting how many suggestions an account can send.

We do not use your information for advertising or to build a profile of you.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. This happens straight away and permanently deletes:

- your account, email address and sign-in details;
- your display name and photo;
- your saved shops, votes and blocked list;
- suggestions of yours that are still waiting for review or were not accepted.

Suggestions that were approved stay in the catalogue, and reports you made stay with the admins, but neither is linked to you any more. Copies in routine backups and server logs are removed on our hosting provider's normal schedule.

If you cannot use the app, email us from your account's email address and we will delete the account for you within 30 days.

## Your choices

- Change or remove your display name and photo in Profile, then Edit Profile.
- Turn location off at any time in your phone's settings; the map still works.
- Block someone to hide their name and photo, and manage the list in Settings.
- Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by email.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights under your local privacy law, including the right to complain to your privacy regulator.

## Children

kopidb is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app before a change that affects how your information is used.
